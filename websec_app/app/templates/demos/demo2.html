↓ bcrypt.verify(password, hash)
2. Si MFA activo → redirige a /auth/mfa
↓ session["pending_user_id"] = uid
3. Usuario → POST /auth/mfa (código TOTP)
↓ pyotp.TOTP(secret).verify(token)

↓ Cookie HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict
